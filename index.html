This isn't just a kick around.

Level Upp Coaching is small-group junior AFL skills coaching for kids aged 8–16. Every session is hands-on and built to give young players real confidence and real game awareness.

We kicked off in November 2025 with our first session at Sacred Heart College Oval in Sorrento. Since then we've run Sunday morning skills sessions, school holiday clinics and weekly term sessions across Perth's northern suburbs. Same idea every time: more touches, more feedback, more fun.

LVL 3 · The Coach

Meet Harry

Head Coach · Level Upp Coaching

Harry has led Level Upp from its very first session at Sacred Heart College Oval in November 2025, with one goal: give young footballers in Perth's northern suburbs coaching that actually makes them better.

His sessions are built on the fundamentals done properly: clean kicking and handballing, smart decisions and real game sense. Keeping groups small means every player gets more touches, more feedback and more chances to back themselves.

He keeps it positive and fun, but he doesn't let the standards slip. Whether it's an 8-year-old in their first season or a 15-year-old chasing their next level, the aim is the same: build skills, build confidence and love your footy.

"Keeps things positive whilst driving high standards."
